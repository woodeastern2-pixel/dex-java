# Jamon Google Play listing — English

## App name

Jamon: Sleep Sounds

## Short description

Build a bedtime routine for tonight and mix 13 calming ambient sounds.

## Full description

When it is time to let the day settle, Jamon prepares a sleep routine around how you feel tonight.

Choose a state such as a busy mind, a tired body or wanting to fall asleep quickly, then set the routine duration yourself. Enter any value from 1 minute to 12 hours, use the up and down controls, or scroll the vertical wheel for precise adjustment.

Preview 13 sleep sounds—including warm brown noise, gentle rain by the window and a soft fan—and combine the ones that feel right. Each sound has its own volume control. After a routine starts, audio keeps playing when the screen turns off or while another app is in use, and you can stop it directly from the notification.

Key features

- Bedtime routines based on how you feel tonight
- Flexible routine duration from 1 minute to 12 hours
- Preview and mix 13 sleep sounds
- Individual volume controls for every sound
- Background playback while the screen is off
- Playback status and stop action in the notification
- Simple weekly routine history stored on the device
- Korean and English language support

Free users can use 5 core sounds and mixes of up to 2 sounds. If you choose to watch and complete a rewarded ad, all 13 sounds and mixes of up to 4 sounds open for 24 hours. Watching an ad is optional. Jamon does not use Google Play Billing, in-app purchases or subscriptions. Ads do not appear on the sleep playback screen or during background playback.

Jamon is not a medical device or medical service and does not diagnose, treat or prevent medical conditions. Its routines and sounds are general wellness features intended to support relaxation and bedtime preparation.
